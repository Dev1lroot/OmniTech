# Fluids

Water, steam, acids, molten metal, fuels — OmniTech moves them all through pipes.

## The Basics

**Fluid Pipes** connect to machines and tanks automatically. Dye a pipe to keep lines of different colours separate.

A **Pump** (needs kinetic force) actively pushes fluid along a line.

A **Fluid Tank** stores a large amount. Right-click it with a bucket or flask to fill or empty it.

A **Valve** opens and closes a line.

A **Fluid Collector** gathers water from rain or the ocean — your first free water supply.

A **Fluid Filler** fills buckets, canisters and flasks from a line.

## Mixing

Fluids that dissolve into each other — water and alcohol, for example — blend into a mixture when they meet in a pipe or tank. Oil and water do not mix, and neither do a gas and a liquid, so keep those on separate lines.

## Temperature and Pressure

Fluids keep their temperature. Very hot water is steam and won't join a line of liquid water. Compressors and decompressors change pressure, and heat exchangers move heat between two streams.

## Cleaning Up

To empty a whole pipe line, **shift + right-click any pipe with an empty hand** — everything connected to it is drained. In a machine window, the small drain button under a tank empties that tank (click it twice to confirm). Flushed fluid is destroyed.

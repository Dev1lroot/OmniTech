# What's Next

Once you can make circuit boards, the rest of the mod opens up.

## Chemistry

The Chemical Mixer, Chemical Reactor, Electrolysis Machine, Solvation Machine, Fractional Distiller and Fermenter turn raw fluids into acids, fuels, solvents and drinks. JEI lists every reaction.

## Computing

The **Logic Machine** is a real RISC-V computer. Flash a program onto a Firmware ROM at the Programming Station — or use a Linux ROM — and connect a Display, Keyboard and Floppy Drive. GPIO Ports let it control redstone.

## Radio

Transmitters, receivers, scanners, microphones and speakers carry voice and data over chosen frequencies.

## Nuclear

A Reactor built from Reactor Blocks, Ports and Cells runs on fuel rods and control rods. Keep it cooled — an overheated reactor explodes.

## Space

Build a rocket, fuel it with hydrazine and fly to the Moon, Mars and beyond.

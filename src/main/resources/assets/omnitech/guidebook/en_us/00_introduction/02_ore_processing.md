# Ore Processing

Vanilla smelting gives one ingot per ore. OmniTech's chain gives you about five.

## Iron, Step by Step

1. **Crush:** one iron ore in the Manual Macerator gives 4 hematite dust.

2. **Spin:** each hematite dust in the Manual Centrifuge gives 1 iron dust and 3 iron motes (plus stone dust).

3. **Combine:** 9 motes craft into 1 dust.

4. **Smelt:** each iron dust smelts into an iron ingot.

Four hematite dust make 4 iron dust and 12 motes — more than five ingots from one ore.

## Other Ores

Every OmniTech mineral works the same way: crush it, then spin it. Many give rare by-products as motes — rare earths, platinum-group metals and more. Check JEI on any dust to see what it spins into.

## By-products Matter

Stone dust, motes and rare dusts all have uses later. Keep a chest for them.

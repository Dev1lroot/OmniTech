# Ore Processing

Raw ores contain significant impurities. OmniTech provides a processing chain that extracts far more metal per ore than vanilla smelting.

![checkerboard test](omnitech:textures/gui/guidebook/example_grid.png){80x80}

## The Processing Chain

**Step 1 — Crush:** Feed ore into the Manual Macerator (powered by a Crank) to produce crushed ore. Each ore block yields two crushed ore.

**Step 2 — Separate:** Put crushed ore into the Manual Centrifuge to separate the metal powder from gangue.

**Step 3 — Smelt:** Smelt the purified metal powder in any furnace. Two powders produce two ingots — double the vanilla yield.

## Alloying

Some materials require combining two or more metals at high temperature. The *Alloy Furnace* handles this and requires a minimum temperature to operate.

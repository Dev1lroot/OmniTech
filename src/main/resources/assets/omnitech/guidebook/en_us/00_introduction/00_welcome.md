# Welcome to OmniTech

OmniTech takes you from wooden cogs and a hand crank all the way to designing your own circuit boards, computers and rockets. Every machine follows real physics: heat flows, fluids mix, circuits are simulated.

## How to Use This Book

Scroll with the mouse wheel, turn pages with the arrows, click blue links to jump. Hold Shift over any OmniTech item for a short description, and use JEI to see every recipe and which machine makes what.

## Your Road

1. Wood age: a crank turns your first machines.

2. Ore processing: crush and spin ores for five times more metal.

3. Heat and metals: heaters, alloys, smelting and casting parts.

4. Kinetic power: engines that turn machines for you.

5. Fluids: pipes, pumps and tanks.

6. Electricity: generators, wires and electric machines.

7. Electronics: silicon, parts and your own circuit boards.

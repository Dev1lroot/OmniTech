# Welcome to OmniTech

OmniTech is a technology progression mod that takes you from primitive stone-age tools all the way to cutting-edge EUV microchip fabrication.

## What You Will Build

Starting with simple hand-cranked machines, you will progress through kinetic power, steam, electricity, chemical processing, logic computing, and space exploration.

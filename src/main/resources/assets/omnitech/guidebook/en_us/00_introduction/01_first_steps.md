# First Steps

Your first goal: get more metal out of every ore. All you need is wood, cobblestone and your own arm.

## 1. Wooden Cogs

Craft a **Wooden Cog**: a plank surrounded by eight sticks. Cogs and **Wooden Reductors** are the gears of every early machine.

## 2. The Crank

Craft a **Crank** (sticks and a wooden cog). A crank powers the block directly **below** it: right-click it and it gives one unit of kinetic force (KF), then spins for a second before you can click again.

## 3. Manual Macerator

Craft a **Manual Macerator** (a grindstone, wooden cogs and cobblestone). Place it, put a crank on top, load an ore and keep cranking. It crushes ore into dust — one iron ore gives four hematite dust. Crushing needs about 20 KF, so about 20 clicks.

## 4. Manual Centrifuge

Craft a **Manual Centrifuge** and crank it the same way. It spins a dust apart: hematite dust becomes iron dust, iron motes and a little stone dust. Spinning needs about 100 KF.

## 5. Smelt the Dust

Iron dust smelts into an iron ingot in any vanilla furnace. Nine motes craft into one dust, so nothing goes to waste.

## Tips

Hand-cranking is slow on purpose. As soon as you can, build a **Kinetic Force Generator** to turn your machines automatically.

A **Kinetic Reductor** under the crank shares its force with every machine connected to it.

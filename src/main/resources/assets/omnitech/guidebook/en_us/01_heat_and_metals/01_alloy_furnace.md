# Alloy Furnace

The Alloy Furnace melts several materials together into an alloy. It has its own fuel slot and heats itself by +1 °C every tick while it burns.

## Your First Steel

1. Put **iron dust** and **coal dust** (crush coal in the Macerator) in the input slots.

2. Add fuel — coal works.

3. Wait. The furnace only burns while a valid recipe is loaded, climbing until it reaches 1500 °C, then it makes steel.

Steel unlocks most of the mid game: plates, rods, cogs and machine casings.

## Watch the Temperature

At **2000 °C the Alloy Furnace melts into lava**. It cools down whenever it isn't burning, so don't leave it overfuelled on a hot recipe and walk away.

## Other Alloys

JEI shows every alloy recipe and its minimum temperature — for example silicon from quartz and coke at 1900 °C.

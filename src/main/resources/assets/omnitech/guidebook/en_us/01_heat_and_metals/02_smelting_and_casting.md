# Smelting and Casting

To make plates, rods, wires, cogs and coils you melt metal and pour it into a shape.

## The Setup

Stack the **Smelter on top of the Foundry**, and put a **Heater** against each of them. The Smelter melts ingots, dusts and scrap into molten metal; the Foundry below pulls it in and casts it.

## Melting Points

- Tin about 232 °C, lead about 400 °C — easy with a coal Heater

- Zinc about 500 °C, aluminium about 700 °C

- Copper about 1100 °C, steel 1500 °C, iron 1600 °C — keep the Heater burning and let the machines climb

## Casting Templates

The Foundry needs a **template** to know what shape to cast. Craft one from bricks and clay around the item you want to copy — an ingot makes an Ingot Template, a plate a Plate Template, and so on. Templates are never used up.

## No Foundry Yet?

Most parts also have plain crafting-table recipes from ingots — they just cost more metal than casting.

## Recycling

Anything metal you melt down comes back as molten metal. Old tools, armour and spare parts are all ore.

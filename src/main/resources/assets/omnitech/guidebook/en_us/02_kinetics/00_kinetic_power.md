# Kinetic Power

Kinetic force (KF) is rotation: it turns the Macerator, Centrifuge, Pump, Press, Filter Press, compressors and more. Cranking by hand works, but engines do it for you.

## Kinetic Force Generator

Your first engine. Put solid fuel in it and while it burns it turns every machine connected to it through Kinetic Shafts.

## Kinetic Shafts and Reductors

**Kinetic Shafts** carry rotation in a line. A **Kinetic Reductor** is a junction: it connects shafts on all sides and lets one engine drive many machines. A crank placed on top of a Reductor drives the whole network.

## Steam Power

For more power, run on steam:

1. A **Boiler** heated by a Heater (or anything hot) boils water into steam.

2. A **Stirling Engine** next to the Boiler takes the steam, turns it into KF, and gives back distilled water — pipe that water back into the Boiler for a closed loop.

## The Electric Engine

Later, an **Electric Engine** turns KF into electricity — or, run in reverse, electricity into KF. See Electricity.
